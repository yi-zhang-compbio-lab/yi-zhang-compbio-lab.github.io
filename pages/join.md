- **We are recruiting postdocs and Duke students**. Interested in computational biology, machine learning, AI, bioinformatics, single-cell multi-omics, spatial transcriptomics, computational pathology, human genetics, and/or immunology? - **Welcome to join us!**

- <u>Postdocs</u> or PhD soon-to-be who work on computational biology, bioinformatics, cancer genomics, single-cell omics, spatial transcriptomics, machine learning, genetic variant function, single-cell epigenetics, and biological questions in tissue microenvironment or immunology - Welcome to contact to join us! Please include (1) CV or Resume (2) A short research statement describing previous/ongoing work and proposed research and interest (3) 1-3 representative publications (published, accepted, or preprint) (4) Three names of references if possible. 
- <u>Postdocs</u> or Duke PhD rotating student who work on multi-modality AI for genomics are welcome to contact Yi for a potential co-advised position with <a href="https://tianlong-chen.github.io" target="_blank">Dr. Tianlong Chen</a> at UNC.
- <u>Prospective students</u> interested in our research are encouraged to apply through Duke graduate programs: Computational Biology and Bioinformatics ([CBB] - PhD), Biostatsistics (PhD & MS), UPGG (PhD). Graduate admission decisions are made by program committee. Open to co-advising. MS students with a quantitative background are welcome to contact for research intern. If contacting for interest, please include (1) CV or Resume (2) Short description of research experience and interest. Thanks!

[CBB]: https://medschool.duke.edu/education/biomedical-phd-programs/computational-biology-and-bioinformatics-program
